# You Are Now Being Chased by Stationery

Some of you finished all 45 levels and told me the game was "relaxing."

Relaxing.

I did not spend weeks drawing spike pits so you could feel *relaxed*. So I hired a villain.

[GIF: devlog2/the-eraser.gif]

## Meet the Eraser

He's a big pink eraser in a white top hat, and he has exactly one hobby: rubbing out everything you love. The world used to be full of color, until he scrubbed it gray and his Washers locked every color away in paint cans. That's why you've been collecting cans this whole time. It was lore all along.

(It was not lore all along. I made it up last week. It works, though.)

There's a new opening comic that tells the whole story in about 30 seconds. You can skip it. He'll remember.

## Boss chases

At the end of every world, right before you get a new color, the Eraser comes for you personally.

[GIF: devlog2/boss-chase.gif]

- **5 boss chases**, from 3½ to almost 7 screens long.
- **The countdown starts the moment the level does.** Standing still to "think" is a strategy. Not a good one.
- **Everything behind him becomes blank paper.** Walls, floors, monsters. You.
- **A red warning on the left** shows how close he is. It's there to help. It doesn't.

[GIF: devlog2/red-sprint.gif]

*Do not look back. He is looking at you.*

My most honest playtester said the first version was "super easy." So the Eraser is now much, much faster. On computers he's **Very Fast**. On phones he's only **Fast**, because I'm not a monster. He is.

## Paint that's actually alive

- 🔵 **Blue is jelly now.** It wobbles when you land on it. Yes, I spent an evening on jelly physics. No regrets.
- 🟢 **Green grows grass, flowers and hanging vines.**
- 🔴 **Red glows and throws sparks**, like it's been angry this whole time.
- 🟡 **Yellow sparkles**, because yellow deserved something nice.

- 👻 **Ghost racing:** race a see-through replay of the #1 run on every level, or your own best.

- 🌤️ **Living backgrounds:** clouds drift, windmills turn, birds fly by. It's basically a nature documentary now.
- 🏅 **43 achievements**, including one called "6, 7...". If you know, you know.
