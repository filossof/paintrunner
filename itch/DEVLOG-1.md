# Paint Runner is out!

The world is black and white. You have the paint.

Paint Runner is a hand-inked puzzle platformer where you can't jump high enough, run fast enough or climb anything on your own. But every surface you paint gains a power:

- 🔵 **Blue** makes it bouncy
- 🔴 **Red** makes you fast
- 🟢 **Green** lets you stick to walls and walk on ceilings
- 🟡 **Yellow** shrinks you (or grows you back)
- 🟣 **Purple** opens portals that keep all your speed

The trick is that monsters follow the same rules. Paint the floor under an Inky and it races, hops, gets glued in place, shrinks or wanders straight into your portal. Hat-wearing monsters shoot paint back at you, and the white-hat Washer scrubs your colors away.

## What's in the game

- **45 levels** across 5 painted worlds, plus a lava-filled finale
- **3 paint cans** in every level, 135 stars to collect
- **No checkpoints.** Every level is a short, sharp run you can master
- **Online leaderboards** timed to the millisecond: fastest time, fastest run with all 3 cans, and fewest throws
- **12 characters** to unlock with stars
- **A cosmetic store** with 40 hats, trails, colors, splats, flags and effects. You pay with Paint Drops you earn by playing. Nothing pay-to-win, no real money.
- **An original soundtrack**

You sign in with Google to play, so your progress follows you to any computer. Leaderboards only show your first name and last initial.
