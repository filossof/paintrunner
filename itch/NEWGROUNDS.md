## Description
The world is black and white. You have the paint.

You can't jump high enough, run fast enough or climb anything by yourself. But every surface you paint gets a power:

🔵 Blue: bounce sky-high
🔴 Red: run at full speed
🟢 Green: stick to walls and walk on ceilings
🟡 Yellow: shrink to squeeze through tiny gaps (or grow back)
🟣 Purple: open two portals and fly through with all your speed

And there's a giant pink Eraser in a top hat who wants the world gray forever. At the end of every world he chases you in person, rubbing out everything behind you. Don't look back.

• 50 hand-inked levels and 5 boss chases
• A Daily Chase: a new level every day, the same for everyone. Race for the top 3.
• Race the ghost of the fastest player on every level
• Leaderboards, achievements, a store full of hats and trails, play streaks
• Mouse + keyboard, or touch on phones and tablets

Controls: A/D or arrows to move, W/Space to jump, aim with the mouse and click to throw paint, 1-5 to switch colors, right-click to wash paint off.

Made by one person (and an AI). Tell me which boss erased you the most!

## Author comments (optional)
Paint Runner started as a "can I make a whole game with AI?" experiment and turned into the thing I can't stop playing. Every level is solvable by a bot, every boss has been tested to death, and the hardest one is still the Great Chase. Good luck.
